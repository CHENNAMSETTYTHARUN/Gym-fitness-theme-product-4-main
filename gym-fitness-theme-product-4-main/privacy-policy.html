01 Overview & Scope

Welcome to Stackly Fitness ("Stackly", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you access our website, enroll in workout or sports training programs, use our digital fitness portal, or interact with our coaching team.

By accessing or using any Stackly Fitness services, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please refrain from using our services or submitting personal information.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver personalized, safe, and effective training experiences:

A. Personal Identification Information

When you create an account, register for a gym membership, or contact us, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Credentials: Securely hashed passwords and unique account identifiers.
  • Billing Information: Payment details, transaction records, and subscription status (processed securely through PCI-DSS certified payment processors).

B. Fitness & Health Profile Data

To tailor athletic regimens and fitness plans to your abilities, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Fitness experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Athlete).
  • Workout preferences (Strength Training, HIIT, Cardio, Sports Performance, Mobility).
  • Physical stats, goals, self-reported activity frequency, and any physical limitations or dietary preferences you choose to share with our certified coaches.

🛡️ Your Health Data is Private

Any fitness assessments or health information you submit is strictly used by our fitness specialists to customize training safely. We never sell or broker your personal health information to third-party advertisers.

C. Device & Technical Information

  • Browser type, operating system version, and IP address.
  • Session timestamps, page views, navigation paths, and referring URLs.

03 How We Use Your Information

We use your data for legitimate fitness service purposes, including:

  • Service Provision: Registering your account, granting access to training programs, scheduling sessions, and tracking membership status.
  • Personalized Coaching: Pairing you with suitable trainers and tailoring workout recommendations to your goals.
  • Communication: Sending session confirmations, gym announcements, training updates, security alerts, and customer support responses.
  • Safety & Optimization: Ensuring platform security, detecting fraud, verifying user identity, and improving website speed and user experience.
  • Marketing (Optional): Sending relevant fitness tips, offers, or event invites, which you can opt out of at any time.

04 Sharing & Third Parties

Stackly Fitness does not sell, lease, or rent your personal data. We only share data under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Reliable infrastructure providers (e.g., secure cloud hosting, payment gateways, transactional email services) bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Certified Trainers & Staff: Authorized Stackly coaches who need relevant profile details to deliver your training programs safely.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by lawful court orders, subpoenas, or to prevent immediate physical harm or fraudulent activity.

05 Data Security & Storage

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: Industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption for data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Credential Protection: Passwords are protected using cryptographic hashing algorithms. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Access Control: Strict role-based permissions ensure that only authorized personnel can access user databases.

While we implement rigorous security measures, please remember that no transmission method across the internet is 100% impenetrable. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and keep your credentials confidential.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We utilize cookies and browser local storage to maintain active login sessions, remember user preferences, and analyze site performance.

  • Essential Cookies: Required to keep you logged in and enable core site navigation and security.
  • Preference Storage: Remembers your display preferences and membership navigation state.

You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies; however, some interactive features of the Stackly Fitness portal may not function properly without them.

07 Your Rights & Choices

You maintain complete control over your personal data. You have the right to:

  • Access & Review: Request a summary of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct your contact information, phone number, or fitness preferences through your account profile or by contacting us.
  • Account Deletion: Request the complete deletion of your account and personal history from our active databases.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing newsletters at any time via the unsubscribe link or account settings.

08 Children's Privacy

Stackly Fitness services are intended for individuals who are 16 years of age or older. For minors (ages 13–15) participating in youth sports training programs, verified consent and registration from a parent or legal guardian is required. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

09 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our gym operations, technical systems, or applicable legal standards. When modifications are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
